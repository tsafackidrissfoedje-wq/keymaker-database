Image generator ID67

 

The ID67 type keys are being used on Toyota vehicles.

 

Allowed transponders:

Any non locked transponders of  the 4D family. It may be a standard ID60, ID61, ID63, ID69 or any other.

The Generator doesn't support emulators (EH2, TPX2 etc.)

 

A programmed transponder may be matched to a car with a original factory procedure and to be used in keymakers.

 

The Page1 is selectable according to the list:

MASTER

MASTER

MASTER

MASTER

Other pages have been calculated automatically.
