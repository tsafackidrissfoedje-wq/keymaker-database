Suzuki Grand Vitara 1996-2002

 

Original transponder

TIRIS 4C

Allowed transponder

TIRIS 4C

Data location

X24C01

No. of  keys

4

 

The distinctive feature of this immo is the rule "Don't make the Master Key".

Usually the immo contains several keys (max 4), one of them is the Master Key. After analyzing of immo dump Tango detects which key

has status of the Master Key and disables making of it.

 

In case Tango cannot detect which key is the Master Key, you will be informed with the message "The Master Key not found. See help.".

To solve this problematic situation, do not make keys on used positions, instead make a key on the not used position.

For example, if  an immo has 2 keys, the Master Key not detected, just make key3.

 

In case the Master Key not detected and you intend to make several keys, but an immo hasn't enough space of not used positions, follow

the rule  "Don't make the First Key".

Example: immo has 2 keys, the Master Key not detected, you intend to make 3 keys. Solution: make key2, key3, key4.
