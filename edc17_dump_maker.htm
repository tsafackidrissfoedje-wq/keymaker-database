Getting started

1.Load EEPROM. Click button Load EEPROM
2.Load FLASH. Click button Load FLASH
3.Decode dump. Click button Decode Dump
4.Edit data (if necessary). Click button Edit near the data that you intend to modify
5.Save your work. Click button Save Dump As
6.Save the decoded data as text (if necessary, e.g. archiving texts). Click button Save Data Text
