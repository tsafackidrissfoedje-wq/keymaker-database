DS1990 (Dallas)

Data location

93C66

No. of  keys

8

 

DS1990 is the 1-wire interfaced tablet chip and not supported by Tango.

To make a key it is needed to read DS1990 with  Orange5, Omega or other device and save a dump.

During making a key Tango will ask for this dump and writes it into the ECU dump.
